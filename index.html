Built for Performance, Security, and True Sovereignty

Unlike traditional financial apps that sell your data, MoneyMap runs directly on your device with local Hive encryption and user-owned Google Drive storage.

User-Owned Google Drive Sync

Backups are compressed and saved directly in your personal Google Drive account as backup.mmbak. No third-party servers ever touch your files.

On-Device Bank SMS Parsing

Optional, user-controlled SMS reader matching strictly whitelisted Indian bank sender IDs. The original raw SMS text is never uploaded or retained after extraction.

Bundled Stocks & Mutual Funds

Integrated offline NSE stock master list and daily mutual fund NAV fetcher. None of your portfolio quantities or asset counts are transmitted to external servers.

Recurring & EMIs Tracking

Automate monthly bills, subscriptions, EMIs, and recurring payments with cashflow forecasts up to 12 months in advance.

Granular Tags & Filters

Organize trips, business expenses, home renovations, and personal projects with multi-dimensional tags and fast filter sheets.

Sub-Millisecond Hive Speed

Powered by high-performance local Hive storage. Zero loading spinners, instant queries, and 100% offline usability.
